Light roles

Every light is main unless you say otherwise. Flow cards can target main or ambient lights separately. Excluded lights are never touched by any card, whatever it targets.

Automatic brightness

Point each room at the variables that already hold its brightness and temperature. The automatic set-card then carries no values of its own — it reads them here.

%

A brightness this low means off, for every card — not just the automatic one. A circadian variable rarely lands on an exact zero, and a bulb held at 3% is a glow nobody asked for. Set it to 0 to turn lights off only at exactly zero.

Daylight

Let the light a room already has move its automatic brightness. Pick where the room reads daylight from, then the two lux values that mean “as dark as it gets” and “as bright as it gets”. Between them the mapped brightness passes through unchanged. Rooms left unset behave exactly as before.

Modelled daylight is computed from the sun's position and this device's cloud cover — it never sees your lamps, so it cannot chase itself. With no device mapped, or once its reading goes stale, the model falls back to a clear sky rather than to darkness.

How far daylight may move the mapped brightness, up or down. Keep it small: a sensor sitting in the room it lights can otherwise chase its own lamps up and down for hours.
